# Hi, I'm Tri Raida

### Entrepreneur | Business & System Analyst | Digitalization

I am an entrepreneur who has spent more than 20 years working with real businesses.

My journey into technology did not start as a developer.

It started with a very practical question:

> **How can technology make running a business easier?**

For years, when I needed a website or software, I worked with developers.

As I became more involved with digital technology, I gradually started to understand not only what software could do, but also **how business processes could be redesigned around it**.

That changed the way I worked.

I started learning, designing, testing, and eventually building applications myself—not because I wanted to become a software engineer, but because I wanted to understand the entire connection between **business problems and digital solutions**.

Today, I describe my role as:

> **Business & System Analyst — bridging business requirements, workflows, and software systems.**

I use technology as a way to understand, improve, and solve business problems.

## How I Think

I don't start with technology.

**I start with the problem.**

My usual thinking process is:

**Business Problem → Process → Requirement → Workflow → System → Information → Decision**

For me, a transaction is not just a record.

A transaction can become information.

Information can trigger an action.

And the result of that action can support the next business decision.

That is the thinking behind many of the systems I build.

## My Approach to Digitalization

I believe digitalization should start with understanding the business.

Not every business problem needs a complicated system.

Sometimes it is automation.

The technology should follow the problem—not the other way around.
