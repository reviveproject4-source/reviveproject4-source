# Hi, I'm Marlina Andriyani,

### Business & System Analyst

I help **small businesses and entrepreneurs** turn everyday business problems into practical digital systems.

I started from business, not from technology. Over the years, I became interested in how business processes can be improved through websites, applications, data, and automation.

What interests me most is the connection between:

**Business → Process → Workflow → Information → Decision**

I don't start with technology.  
**I start with the problem.**

## How I Think About Business Systems

A business system should do more than store data.

For example:

**Transaction → Information → Action → Next Decision**

A transaction should help a business answer:

- What happened?
- What needs to happen next?
- What needs attention?
- Who should take action?
- What information is needed for the next decision?

This way of thinking influences how I design workflows and software systems.
